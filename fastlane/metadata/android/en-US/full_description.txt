A local-only video player for a toddler. The feel of YouTube Kids, a grid of big
tappable thumbnails, but it only ever shows video files from one folder you
choose, and it has no way to reach the internet.

The manifest declares zero permissions. Without INTERNET the app cannot open a
socket at all. Storage is a single persisted Storage Access Framework grant to
one folder you nominate, so the app can read that folder and nothing else on the
device. There is no delete, rename, move or edit code path anywhere in the app,
no ads, no in-app purchases, and no third-party code of any kind.

The parent gate behind the gear icon needs a continuous two second press,
deliberately longer than the platform long-press a toddler finds by accident.
Behind it: the video folder, whether to include subfolders, what happens when a
video ends, sort order, grid size, the PIN, and instructions for Android's
screen pinning. Five wrong PIN entries lock the keypad for thirty seconds.

There is no seek bar in the player, only a thin non-interactive progress line,
and cards have no long-press behaviour, so there is nothing to trigger by
accident.
